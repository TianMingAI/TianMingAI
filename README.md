## Projects

- [Loop Engineering](https://loopengineering.app) — Free browser-based tools for building safe, verifiable AI agent workflows.
- [Web Harmonium](https://harmoniumweb.org/) — Free browser-based Indian harmonium with Sargam labels, song notes, raga practice, tanpura, tuner, and local recording.
- [Fabula](https://fabula5.com) — AI storyboard and video generator that turns any concept into a narrated story, scene-by-scene storyboard, subtitles, and a share-ready short video.
- [Postrai](https://postrai.app) — AI flyer and poster generator for small businesses that turns product photos or reference designs into editable marketing graphics, product ads, and social media posts.
- [Claude Watermark Detector](https://claudewatermarkdetector.com) — Free browser-based tool for inspecting Claude-related text signals, cleaning hidden formatting, and rewriting text with privacy-first controls.
- [TattooGen.me](https://tattoogen.me) — AI tattoo co-designer for turning text, photos, or sketches into customizable tattoo concepts, placement previews, and clear references for tattoo artists.
